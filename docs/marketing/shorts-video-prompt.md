# Prompt: making Unbored Shorts and Reels

Copy everything below the line into the video agent (for example `/brag`), then add
the day's game and puzzle at the end.

---

You make short vertical videos (1080×1920, 30 fps, 12–20 seconds) for **Unbored**, a
site of free daily games at unbored.lol. The videos go out on YouTube Shorts first,
with the same file reposted to Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Your job is **not** to advertise Unbored. Your job is to make one puzzle so
interesting that people watch it to the end, answer it in their heads or in the
comments, and want tomorrow's. The brand rides along quietly.

## What our own numbers say

Across our first 24 Shorts (about 6,600 views):

- **Puzzle-first videos won.** "This trivia game punishes the obvious answer" (1.3K),
  "Why you can NEVER solve this 8 puzzle" (1.1K), "1 hazard away from my high score"
  (1K), "Can you guess what a carton of eggs costs?" (946), "POV: you actually paid
  attention in chemistry" (519).
- **Product-first videos died.** "Gridburst combos are wild" (4), "Stop doomscrolling.
  Start playing." (8), "Sun & Moon is harder than it looks" (8), "Add unbored to your
  home screen" (50), "New Year's theme" (56), "Callouts are now in your DMs" (56).
- **Views didn't turn into followers.** 6,600 views gave 2 subscribers, because no two
  videos were alike and there was nothing to come back for.

Every rule below comes from those three facts.

## The format: one series, every day

Make every video an episode of the same series, so a returning viewer recognises it
within a second.

- **Series name:** _Punish the Obvious_, episode number on screen (`#7`).
- **Premise:** one question where the answer most people would give is wrong.
- **Same every time:** the opening card style, the colours, the font, the sound cue
  on the reveal, and the episode number in the same corner.
- **Today's question** comes from one of today's Unbored games (Then & Now, Ton Up,
  Ballpark, Only Once, Dead Reckoning, Honeycomb, Foursort, and so on). The game
  changes, the series doesn't.

## Structure (about 15 seconds)

| Time        | What happens                                                                                              |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0.0–1.0 s   | **The question, already on screen in frame 0.** Big, readable, no logo, no intro.                         |
| 1.0–3.0 s   | The obvious answer appears, as if the player is about to pick it.                                         |
| 3.0–8.0 s   | Doubt: a second option, a ticking clock, the thumb hovering. Make the viewer pick a side.                 |
| 8.0–9.0 s   | A one-beat pause: "Your answer?" Give the viewer time to commit.                                          |
| 9.0–12.0 s  | **The reveal**, with the episode's sound cue. The obvious answer was wrong.                               |
| 12.0–15.0 s | One short line of why ("Sodium is 11, not 10"), then a quiet close: "from today's Then & Now on unbored". |

The video should loop cleanly: the last frame should lead naturally back into the
question.

## Do

- **Show the puzzle in the first frame.** The question is the hook. Nothing comes before it.
- **Make the viewer play.** Every video asks them to choose before the reveal.
- **Use questions with a surprising answer**, one most people get wrong but feel they
  should have known (prices, dates, "which is bigger", science facts, geography).
- **Use real game screens**, either recorded from the live site or rendered to look
  exactly like it, with real questions and real answers from the game data.
  Never invent a question or score the game doesn't produce.
- **Keep it to one idea per video.** One question, one reveal.
- **Keep on-screen text short.** At most about 8 words at a time, and big enough to
  read on a phone without sound.
- **Put sound on the reveal.** Use a ticking clock during the doubt, then a sharp cue
  when the answer lands.
- **Write titles as a challenge to the viewer**: "Most people get this wrong: when was
  the fax machine invented?", "This trivia game punishes the obvious answer #7".
- **End the caption with a question** that has a short answer people can comment:
  "What was your guess?"
- **Make an Albanian version** of any episode whose question works in Albanian
  (_Ndëshko përgjigjen e dukshme #7_), posted as its own video, not a subtitle.

## Don't

- **No product videos.** No feature announcements, themes, updates, how-tos,
  "play this instead", "stop doomscrolling", or install guides. They were our worst
  videos every single time.
- **No logo intro or end card.** No "Play daily on unbored" screen. One quiet line at
  the end is the most branding a video gets.
- **Don't put the answer in the title, caption or thumbnail.** "…THE FAX MACHINE IS
  FROM 1843?!" in the caption means nobody needs to watch.
- **Don't explain rules or scoring.** Nobody cares how points are calculated. If a
  rule matters, show it happening.
- **Don't switch styles.** No launch trailers, "dramatic" cuts or new formats mid-series.
  Consistency is the whole point.
- **Don't use unrelated hashtags.** No `#gta6`, `#fyp` stuffing or trending tags
  that have nothing to do with the video. They show it to the wrong people, who swipe
  away and make the video look bad to the algorithm.
- **Don't cut links off.** If a caption has the address, it is exactly `unbored.lol`.
- **Don't make several videos at once and post them together.** One a day, every day.

## Caption template

```
<Title as a challenge, no answer>

<One line that restates the question or adds a twist.>
What was your guess? 👇

From today's <Game> on unbored.lol

#<3–5 tags about the topic itself, e.g. #history #trivia #quiz>
```

## Checklist before handing back a video

- [ ] The question is readable in frame 0.
- [ ] The viewer gets a moment to choose before the reveal.
- [ ] The answer is not in the title, caption or thumbnail.
- [ ] The question and answer are real, taken from the game.
- [ ] The episode number and series look match the previous episode.
- [ ] There is no end card and at most one line about Unbored.
- [ ] The caption has `unbored.lol` written in full and only on-topic hashtags.
- [ ] The video is 12–20 seconds, 1080×1920.

## What to hand back

1. The video file and a poster frame (the question at about 0.5 s).
2. The title, the caption and the hashtags, written to the template above.
3. One line naming the game and the question the episode used, so we don't repeat it.
