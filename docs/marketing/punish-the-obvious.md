# Punish the Obvious: series look and episode log

The fixed look for every episode of the daily Shorts series (brief: `shorts-video-prompt.md`). Episode #1's build (`brag-output-2026-11-21-162510/work/video.html` and `synth.js`) is the reference: copy it and change only the question, the game screen and the episode number.

## Look (never changes)

- 1080×1920, 30 fps, app dark background `oklch(0.175 0.006 270)`, Geist, light film grain.
- Top-left: orange dot + **PUNISH THE OBVIOUS** (34 px, weight 700, letter-spacing .12em, primary orange `oklch(0.7 0.18 40)`).
- Top-right: episode pill **#N** (64 px, weight 800, white, 3 px white outline, 22 px radius).
- Question: white, 112 px, weight 750, tight tracking, top of the frame, on screen from frame 0 to the last frame.
- One caption line under it (62 px, at most about 4 words), and an orange clock bar that drains during the doubt.
- Options: orange round badges **A** / **B** on the real game screen.
- "Your answer?" beat (8–9 s): the game screen dims, the words land centre.
- Reveal at 9.0 s: wrong option turns red ✕, right option green ✓, white flash, small camera jolt.
- **The sound cue ("the verdict"):** a low thud on the downbeat plus a two-note glass chime, B5 then E6 a 16th later. Woodblock tick on every beat through the doubt, 8ths in the last bar, near-silence on "Your answer?".
- Music: 120 BPM, E minor, cuts on beats.
- Close: one muted line, `from today's <Game> on unbored`. No logo, no end card. The last second returns to the frame-0 layout so it loops.
